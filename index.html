AI · MSc Thesis · 2025–Present
CardioBase ECG Diagnostics AI

A 1D Convolutional Neural Network trained on cardiologist-classified ECG datasets to detect acute myocardial infarction (AMI) — the second leading cause of death in Portugal. The model integrates directly into CardioBase, deployed across 20+ hospitals, aiming to assist diagnosis in facilities without on-call cardiologists. Handling the full ML pipeline: dataset curation, patient data anonymisation, model training, fine-tuning, and evaluation using sensitivity (recall) and F1 score to minimise false negatives.
